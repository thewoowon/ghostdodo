# 07. Reputation, Credits & Community

## 1. Two separate systems

### Reputation
Long-lived trust/standing. Cannot be bought or transferred.

### Dodo Credit
Contribution currency / product utility. Not cash, not crypto, no speculative language.

Keep them separate.

## 2. Reputation rewards

Reward:
- complete structured report
- evidence verified
- report corroborated by independent users
- helpful factual correction
- adding missing company metadata accurately
- flagging PII successfully
- high-quality community answers
- maintaining a low reversal/moderation rate

Do NOT reward:
- negative sentiment
- number of companies accused
- sensational free text
- coordinated mass reports

## 3. Suggested rank system

Use original names and bespoke SVG badges.

```text
관찰자
탐사자
기록자
검증자
아키비스트
도도 레인저
```

Do not make rank depend only on raw point total. Add trust criteria at higher tiers.

## 4. Badge examples

- 첫 기록
- 검증된 기록 10회
- 팩트체커
- 정정 기여
- 30일 연속 유용 기여 (avoid addictive dark patterns)
- 다양한 업종 관측
- 응답 우수 기업 발견

“First Discoverer” must only mean first qualified structured observation, not “first person to accuse.”

## 5. Dodo Credit uses

Possible non-monetary uses:
- profile cosmetic unlocks
- custom field-guide cover theme
- limited extra saved-company slots (avoid punishing free users)
- community flair
- early beta feature access

Do not gate safety, correction, or essential transparency behind credits.

## 6. Community design

### Feed ranking
Prefer:
- recent + useful
- report-linked context
- followed companies

Do not optimize purely for outrage engagement.

### Voting
Use `도움됐어요` rather than upvote/downvote for experience cards.

### Identity
Public pseudonym by default. Real identity never required to be public.

### Company discussions
Company can be tagged. Recruiter/interviewer personal names are prohibited.

## 7. Profile page

Show:
- handle
- reputation rank + custom SVG badge
- verified contribution count
- helpfulness
- industries observed
- badges
- joined date

Do not publicly show exact companies a user applied to unless they explicitly opt in per item.

## 8. Viral mechanics

Shareable profile card:

```text
GhostDodo 기록자
검증 기여 24건
팩트체커 배지
```

Shareable company card should be more important than user leaderboard.

Avoid public “worst company reporter” leaderboards.
